# A map change with a network client (docs/pc_multiplayer.md "Map changes"): the host's match (de_aztec, two bots, one
# round of a minute, mp_maxrounds 1) ends after its round and the title relaunches into the next map, as every map
# change does in this build (changelevel is disabled: "E3 HACK"). The host tells the client (the transport's
# MapChange), the client relaunches into "+connect" and joins the new map. Shows: the server's relaunch keeps its port
# (the sockets close on exec), the client rejoins by itself, both run the new map.
# timeout: 600
[host] --launch tests/launch/de_aztec_maxrounds1 --env FPS=30
wait log open D:\\(de|cs)_[a-z0-9_]*\.sxwad
wait draws>=150
sleep 5
press A
sleep 4
press B
wait log net:.client.127\.0\.0\.2:[0-9]+.connected
wait log net:.relaunching.into.de_[a-z0-9_]+:.the.clients
wait log relaunching
wait log open D:\\(de|cs)_[a-z0-9_]*\.sxwad
wait log net:.server.endpoint.on.UDP.port.27015
wait log net:.client.127\.0\.0\.2:[0-9]+.connected
wait log entered.the.game
sleep 20
players newmap
expect playercount newmap 2 70
expect nolog guest trap
expect nolog cannot.open
[client] --after host:Round_Start --launch tests/launch/connect_local --env FPS=30
wait log net:.server.127\.0\.0\.1:27015.connected
wait log pc.keys:.menu.TeamSelect
sleep 2
press A
sleep 4
press B
wait log net:.relaunching.to.rejoin
wait log net:.connect.127\.0\.0\.1:27015
wait log net:.server.127\.0\.0\.1:27015.connected
wait log pc.keys:.menu.TeamSelect
sleep 2
press A
sleep 4
press B
sleep 20
expect nolog guest trap
expect nolog Host_Error
