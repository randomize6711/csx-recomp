# N7 gameplay (docs/n7_gameplay.md, src/game/pc_gameplay.cpp), headless: de_aztec, no bots, the match line's
# "+mp_flashlight 1 +pc_gravity 400", PC Options > Interface > Flashbang Effect Black. The gravity is set on the
# server; the flashlight (impulse 100, the binding's command) turns on and off on the server; held looking down
# (tools/drive.py setview), the spray (impulse 201) sprays a logo on the floor (the server's spray, its next spray
# time set; a snap shows what the client draws) and a flashbang bought (buyitem) and thrown at the player's feet
# blinds it with the fade drawn black.
#   --env FPS=30 --pc flashbang_colour=black --launch tests/launch/de_aztec_n7
wait log open D:\\(de|cs)_[a-z0-9_]*\.sxwad
count pc.gameplay:.gravity.400 1
wait draws>=150
sleep 10
press DOWN
sleep 1
press DOWN
sleep 1
press A
sleep 4
press B
sleep 14
pckey cmd impulse 100
count pc.gameplay:.*'s.flashlight.on 1
sleep 1
pckey cmd impulse 100
count pc.gameplay:.*'s.flashlight.off 1
pckey cmd buyitem weapon_flashbang
sleep 2
setview 2352 -256 -251.969 135 80
sleep 1
pckey cmd impulse 201
count pc.gameplay:.*.sprayed.a.logo 1
sleep 1
snap spray
pckey cmd weapon_flashbang
sleep 2
pckey cmd +attack
sleep 0.3
pckey cmd -attack
count pc.gameplay:.a.flashbang's.white.fade.drawn.black 1
setview off
expect nolog guest trap
