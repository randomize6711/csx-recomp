# The buy menu's tap (FINDINGS.md "Buy menu taps"): the title opens the buy menu on +buymenu's release only when it
# was held 0.05 to 0.5 s of real time. drive.py's `pckey tap` has the game hold a command for a set time on its own
# clock (pc_keys.cpp, the hold logged and checked), pressed right after the HUD's half-second settings check, whose
# commands would otherwise join the title's reply "xbui buymenu" (no line end). At 30, 60, 120 and 240 frames a
# second (the Frame Rate Limit changed while playing): taps of 0.08 and 0.35 s open the buy menu (the right button
# closes it), a hold of 0.7 s does not. On an empty de_dust2 after the title's own buy menu at spawn has closed.
#   --env FPS=30 --pc frame_rate=30 --launch tests/launch/de_dust2_nobots

sleep 4

sleep 12

sleep 24
