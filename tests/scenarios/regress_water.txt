# de_aztec's water headless at 4:3 (FINDINGS.md "Water"): its pixel shader makes the water's alpha from a cube
# texture on stage 0 (the title's D3D turns the stage's 2D mode into the cube mode for a cube texture), a 32x32 cube
# whose texel along V is V |V_major| / |V|^2, sampled along eye - P (the vertex program's t0): r = its z (the
# water's normal is up), alpha = 0.102 r + 0.302 (1 - r), more opaque at grazing angles. The player held over the
# river (tools/drive.py setview, no bots) in three views, looking along it (12 degrees down), steeply down (70) and
# back the other way (25): the alpha of the water's draws at a pixel 200 columns right of the centre and 160 rows
# below it (of the 960; the same ray at every aspect, Hor+; water in all three views) is the cube's (graze 0.244,
# steep 0.157, back 0.230; a unit normalisation cube would give 0.227, 0.132, 0.194 and no cube 0.302), and what
# lies under the water shows the water as much as the reference renderer and Vulkan agree on (mean chroma of a box
# of water). (Auto Exposure off, so the frames compare directly.)
#   --env FPS=30 --env PROBES=520,640 --env PROBE_WIDTH=640 --pc auto_exposure=0 --pc aspect=4:3
