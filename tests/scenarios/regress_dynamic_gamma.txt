# Auto exposure headless (FINDINGS.md "Dynamic gamma", src/game/dynamic_gamma.cpp, SDK patch 0030): the reference
# renderer stores the title's 40x60 LIN_L8 luminance measurement one byte a pixel and draws it on every frame, so after
# a drawn frame (a snap) the title measures a picture (the ramp had sunk to black on RGB565 noise and cleared
# targets) and its ramp settles on it; PC Options' Auto Exposure Off stops the measurement and sets the title's
# identity ramp, On starts it again.
#   --env FPS=30 --env CSX_LOG_DYNGAMMA=1 --launch tests/launch/de_dust2_nobots
wait log open D:\\(de|cs)_[a-z0-9_]*\.sxwad
expect log pc: Auto Exposure on
wait draws>=150
sleep 10
press DOWN
sleep 1
press DOWN
sleep 1
press A
sleep 4
press B
sleep 12
snap spawn
expect image spawn 20
sleep 15
exposure settled
expect exposure settled mean 0.1 0.5
expect exposure settled high 0.37 0.40
expect exposuretrack settled 0.05
snap spawn2
expect region spawn2 0 0 640 480 40 200
pckey set auto_exposure 0
sleep 2
expect log pc: Auto Exposure off, the title's identity ramp set
expect quiet dynamic.gamma: 3
pckey set auto_exposure 1
sleep 1
exposure again
expect exposure again high 0.37 0.40
