# Native split-screen M3.2 (docs/native_splitscreen.md 17) in the Vulkan window: two local players in one process
# PLAYING, not watching. Player 1 chooses its team with its own menus (the shared front end's driver), player 2 by the
# development auto-join (its own "menuselect" lines through its own client; CSX_NATIVE_SPLIT_TEAMS=1,2: player 2 a
# Counter-Terrorist, player 1 a Terrorist when it chooses automatically after its rejoin; random classes; one a team, so
# no team balance moves anyone), then a round restart (the server's own sv_restartround) spawns both. Each moves and aims
# through its own input only: player 1 the mouse (look) and the keyboard (W), player 2 its controller (the test
# controller on port 1: CSX_NATIVE_SPLIT_TEST_PAD=1, its right stick and left stick); the server's positions and view
# angles (players steps: origin, health, v_angle) prove that each input moved its own player and not the other.
# 60 s after player 2 joined, player 1 leaves once and rejoins 10 s later, chooses its team automatically (no
# longer the driver), and a restart spawns it again. de_dust2 with no bots, 30 fps, the server write guard on.
# Window shots (RUN/shots): gp_spawned, gp_p1_aims, gp_p2_aims, gp_p2_moved, gp_both, gp_alone, gp_rejoined.
# Run with tools/run_window_tests.py (gamescope --backend headless -W 1920 -H 1080), with
