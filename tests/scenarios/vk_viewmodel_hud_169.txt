# title draws the HUD after the 3D view with the depth test on (LEQUAL at depth 0, no depth writes); the view's cull
# range is MinZ -1 to MaxZ 1, so what lies 2 to 4 units from the eye (the weapon, in the frame at a wide FOV) is kept

# clamped to 0 to 1 (VK_EXT_depth_clamp_control) after the cull range's clip (VK_EXT_depth_clip_enable).
