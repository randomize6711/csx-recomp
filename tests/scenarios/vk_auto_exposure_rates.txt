# Auto exposure at 30, 120 and 240 frames per second (FINDINGS.md "Dynamic gamma"): the title steps its ramp by the
# frame time, so it settles on the same points and moves as far in a second at every rate; the late copy of its
# measurement never makes the frame wait (the rates hold). The Frame Rate Limit changed while playing (pckey set, as
# Options > Video's Accept), VSync off for 240. At each rate: level view, then the floor (one second and eight seconds
# after looking down), then level again. The look down is about 60 degrees (26 x 35 counts, 0.066 degree a count), inside
# the 89-degree clamp, so the same stream up returns to level: a stream past the clamp (26 x 80) came back 48 degrees
# up, and the level views at 120 and 240 were of the sky against level30's horizon (+0.018 "middle" from the view,
# not the rate; N4.7). Every measurement records its view; the level views and the floor views are checked to match.
# Run with tools/run_window_tests.py (gamescope --backend headless -W 1280 -H 960 -r 120), with
