# Mouse look and Raw Mouse Input, the Vulkan window with SDL motion events pushed from a timer thread at 500-2000 a
# second (they arrive between the window's polls, as a mouse's do) (FINDINGS.md "Raw mouse input", src/linux/
# window_input.cpp, src/linux/window_main.cpp, src/game/mouse_look.cpp). Raw Mouse Input is on with no setting and
# relative mode is native (logged with its path). The same counts turn the view by exactly counts x 0.022 x the
# sensitivity, slow (0.5 a motion), medium (2) and fast (10), at 240 (VSync off), 144, 120, 60 and 30 frames per
# second: no acceleration, smoothing, frame time or lost or doubled motion; at 240 every look pass turns about the same
# (the window waits on events: read in 5 ms batches some frames had none and the next two). Sensitivity 1.5 halves
# the turn, Invert Mouse flips the pitch, full screen changes nothing. Motion over the pause menu and the open console
# does not turn the view, nor does any after them. Raw Mouse Input Off from the pause menu's PC Options is saved and
# applied at once (SDL's relative system scale: the desktop's pointer motion; the pushed counts are not the desktop's,
# so they turn the same), and On again.
