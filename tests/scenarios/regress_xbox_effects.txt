# Options > Video > Xbox Graphical Effects, headless (FINDINGS.md "Xbox Graphical Effects", src/common/pc_settings.cpp
# pc::XboxEffect): the master over the two optional effects, Bloom (cl_hdrbloom) and Auto Exposure (cl_dynamicgamma).
# Started with the master Off saved and Bloom and Auto Exposure On saved: both are off from the map's first frame
# (the map's 0.5 and 1 taken back after its light config) while their own saved values stay 1. Then all eight
# combinations of the three settings, changed while playing at one held view (tools/drive.py setview, no bots): the
# cvars follow master AND setting (`expect effects`, CSX_LOG_EFFECTS=1), no setting is rewritten by another, the
# adjustment runs only with the master and Auto Exposure On (CSX_LOG_DYNGAMMA logs one a second while it does), and
# every frame with the master Off equals the frame with the master On and both effects Off (the identity ramp, no
# bloom), whatever the effects' own settings; the bloom brightens the sky (the upper right) only with both On.
#   --env FPS=30 --env CSX_LOG_DYNGAMMA=1 --env CSX_LOG_EFFECTS=1 --pc xbox_effects=0 --pc bloom=1
#   --pc auto_exposure=1 --launch tests/launch/de_aztec_nobots

expect log pc: the map's light config set cl_hdrbloom 0.5, Bloom off sets 0
