# Native split-screen M3 (docs/native_splitscreen.md 16) in the Vulkan window: one process, the built-in server, two
# local players, each drawing its own view in its own turn (its SCR_UpdateScreen), placed in its half of one window
# (player 1 top, player 2 bottom) and presented once a window frame. Player 1's view is turned 90 degrees by its mouse
# (player 1's own) and player 2's is not (camera isolation). 30 s after player 2 joins player 1 leaves through its own
# "disconnect" (once: CSX_NATIVE_SPLIT_LEAVE_TIMES=1); player 2 then draws the whole window (no blank screen), and 10 s
# later player 1 rejoins in its kept context and the two views come back. de_dust2 with no bots, the guard on.
# Window shots (RUN/shots): ns_two (both views), ns_turned (player 1 turned), ns_alone (player 2 alone), ns_again.
# Run with tools/run_window_tests.py (gamescope --backend headless -W 1920 -H 1080), with
#   --launch tests/launch/de_dust2_nobots --env FPS=30 --env CSX_NATIVE_SPLIT=2 --env CSX_CTX_GUARD=1 --env CSX_NATIVE_SPLIT_CHANGES=1 --env CSX_NATIVE_SPLIT_ROLLBACK=0 --env CSX_NATIVE_SPLIT_LEAVE_AFTER=30 --env CSX_NATIVE_SPLIT_LEAVE_PLAYER=1 --env CSX_NATIVE_SPLIT_LEAVE_TIMES=1 --env CSX_NATIVE_SPLIT_REJOIN_AFTER=10
