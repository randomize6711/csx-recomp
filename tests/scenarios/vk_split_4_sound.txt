# Four split-screen players' audio (docs/splitscreen.md 8): every player's own stream at full Master Volume through
# SDL to its dummy device (SDL_AUDIO_DRIVER=dummy: nothing reaches the speakers, nothing is muted) and captured as the
# device gets it (CSX_AUDIO_WAV, each in its own folder), the game sounds each process starts logged (CSX_LOG_SOUNDS,
# CSX_LOG_MENU_SOUNDS). Four players and 3 bots on de_airstrip: each player's pause menu (menu sounds), gunfire (three
# shots each), a reload, footsteps, radio (coverme, enemyspot), player 3 firing beside player 2 with player 2 facing
# one way and then the other (positional), player 4 killed (its death, then spectator audio), a map change (the match's one round over: the next map;
# the captures go on) and gunfire after it. tools/split_audio.py build/wruns/vk_split_4_sound then reads the four
# streams: levels, peaks, clipping in each and in their sum, late callbacks, each sound heard in its own stream,
# duplicates, left and right as expected.
# Run with tools/run_window_tests.py (gamescope --backend headless -W 1920 -H 1080), with
#   --env CSX_SPLIT_COMPOSITOR=1 --env CSX_SPLIT_PLAYERS=4 --env CSX_SPLIT_ZCACHE=$PWD/build/zcache --env CSX_AUDIO=sdl
#   --env SDL_AUDIO_DRIVER=dummy --env CSX_AUDIO_WAV=audio.wav --env CSX_LOG_SOUNDS=1 --env CSX_LOG_MENU_SOUNDS=1
