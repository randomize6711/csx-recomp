# back buffer's colour above half, 2c - 1, and the title draws dark for the gamma ramp to brighten). Started with

# glow brightens the upper right quarter of the frame (before SDK patch 0034 the bloom's last pass kept the scissor
# of its small targets and drew in the top left corner only: no change there); the back buffer's downsample is
# sampled with GAUSSIANCUBIC (the 3x3 Gaussian, patch 0035). Then a flashbang with Bloom On: part way through its fade
# the frame's top left quarter by a sixteenth of its height is not brighter than the rest (the corner the leaked
# scissor brightened by 10 to 32 levels). Auto Exposure Off from the start: the map's cl_dynamicgamma 1 is taken back
# to 0 and no adjustment runs (CSX_LOG_DYNGAMMA logs one a second while it does; FINDINGS.md "Auto Exposure at the
# start").
