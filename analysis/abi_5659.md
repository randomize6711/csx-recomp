# LTCG [abi] maps for XDK 5659 D3D8LTCG (Counter-Strike default.xbe)

Source: disassembly of `build/ghidra/default_flat.exe` (llvm-objdump). Every map is proven from the callee's use of
each register/stack slot and its `ret` size, and from at least one caller's setup. Argument order and count follow the
runtime's HLE declarations in `sdk/runtime/xdk/d3d8.cpp`. BLACK's maps were consulted only as hints.

Result: **15 of 15 proven, 0 unproven. All 15 candidate addresses are confirmed; none is wrong.**

`D3DDevice_*` functions read the device from `D3D_pDevice` (`[0x316FE8]`). Offsets that match 5849 `layout.toml`
are noted as identity evidence.

## Proofs

**CDevice_KickOff (this) — 0x30A2C0.** Identity: it uses the 5849 signature constants 0x770, 0x1C20 and 0x1DE4, plus
SwapCount 0x2478 at 0x30A354. At 0x30A2DC–0x30A2E8 it writes the push pointer to the hardware put register at
`[[edx+0x1C20]+0x40]`.
- Callee: edx is the device. 0x30A2C0 reads `[edx+8]` (flags) and 0x30A2D0 reads `[edx]` (push pointer). No stack
  read; plain `ret` (0x30A327, 0x30A361).
- Callers: 0x197481 and 0x1979C9 both do `mov edx, [0x316FE8]` then `call 0x30A2C0`.
- HLE_FASTCALL(…, 0) accepts a one-entry map.

**D3DDevice_DrawVertices (PrimitiveType, StartVertex, VertexCount) — 0x3083B0.** Identity: emits NV097 BEGIN_END
(0x417FC) and DRAW_ARRAYS (0x40001810 | …).
- eax = VertexCount: kept in edi (0x3083BD). It drives the 256-vertex batching at 0x3083D1 and 0x30841C, and is
  written as `(count-1)<<24` at 0x308451.
- ecx = StartVertex: kept in ebx (0x3083BF). Written as the DRAW_ARRAYS start at 0x308434/0x308455, and advanced by
  0x100 per batch (0x308442).
- stack = PrimitiveType: `[esp+0x14]` at 0x308409, after 4 pushes, is the first slot. It is written after BEGIN_END
  at 0x308422. `ret 4` at 0x308472.
- Caller 0xAF547: `push 8` (primitive), `mov eax,4` (count), `xor ecx,ecx` (start).

**D3DDevice_SelectVertexShader (Handle, Address) — 0x3073D0.**
- eax = Handle: tested at 0x3073D0; `handle-1` is stored to device+0x794 at 0x3073F7. 0x794 is
  CDevice_VertexShader in 5849.
- ebx = Address: written as the argument of method 0x41EA0 (transform program start) at 0x307474, and stored to
  device+0x79C at 0x30747C. Plain `ret` at 0x307483.
- Callers: 0x307B07/0x307B0B (inside SetVertexShader 0x307AD0) load eax = handle argument and `xor ebx,ebx`.
  0x307376–0x30738F sets eax = 0x318088|1 and ebx = incoming eax.

**D3DDevice_SetIndices (pIndexData, BaseVertexIndex) — 0x306610.**
- ebx = pIndexData: AddRef `+0x80000` at 0x306621; `[ebx+4]` is stored to 0x317E98 at 0x30662A; stored to device+0x7A0
  at 0x306661. 0x7A0 is CDevice_IndexBuffer in 5849.
- stack = BaseVertexIndex: `[esp+8]` at 0x306613, after one push, is the first slot. Stored to device+0x1C at 0x30665E.
  0x1C is CDevice_BaseVertex in 5849. `ret 4` at 0x306669.
- Callers: 0x196648 `mov ebx,[ebp+8]` / `push 0`; 0x19835C `mov ebx,[0x5437CC]` / `push 0`.

**D3DDevice_SetPixelShader (Handle) — 0x304F70.**
- eax = Handle: stored to device+0x784 at 0x304F84. 0x784 is CDevice_PixelShader in 5849. 0x304F98 ors 0x4800 into
  `[0x316FF0]`. Plain `ret` at 0x304FF1.
- Callers: 0x12AA8 `mov eax,[ebx+0x638]`; 0x15B9F `mov eax,[0x4DF180]`. Nothing pushed.

**D3DDevice_SetPixelShaderConstant (Register, pConstantData, ConstantCount) — 0x305180.**
- ecx = Register: kept in edi (0x30518F). Used as the constant index (0x3051FD, 0x305226) and incremented per
  constant at 0x30542E.
- stack = pConstantData: `[ebp+8]` is read as 4 floats (`movups` at 0x3051B4) and advanced by 0x10 at 0x30542B.
- eax = ConstantCount: zero test at 0x305186 skips the loop; saved at 0x3051AD; decremented as the loop counter at
  0x30542F/0x305436.
- `ret 4` at 0x305442.
- Callers: 0x12AC8 `push ebx+0x63C`, `eax=8`, `ecx=0`. 0x15E17 `push 0x3711E8`, `eax=1`, `ecx=0`; then 0x15E2D
  `push 0x3711F8`, `eax=1`, `ecx=1`.

**D3DDevice_SetShaderConstantMode (Mode) — 0x307490.**
- eax = Mode: 0x307490 tests bit 0x10 (D3DSCM_NORESERVEDCONSTANTS, also what the HLE tests) and sets or clears
  device flag 0x200. The rest is stored to device+0x1928 at 0x3074B2. Plain `ret` at 0x3075B3.
- Callers: 0xE04B3 `xor eax,eax`; 0x194F90 `mov eax,1`.

**D3DDevice_SetStreamSource (StreamNumber, pStreamData, Stride) — 0x3070C0.**
- eax = StreamNumber: indexes the stream table by eax×12 (0x3070D6–0x3070D9). 12 is Stream_Size in 5849.
- ebx = pStreamData: AddRef at 0x3070D0; stored to table+8 (0x317FD0) at 0x30711D. +8 is Stream_Buffer in 5849.
- stack = Stride: `[esp+8]` at 0x3070C9, after one push. Stored to table+0 (0x317FC8) at 0x307117; +0 is
  Stream_Stride. `ret 4` at 0x30712F/0x30713C.
- Callers: 0xAF504–0xAF538 `mov ebx,[0x55361C]`, `push 0x18`, `xor eax,eax`; 0xAF571 `push 0`, `xor ebx,ebx`,
  `xor eax,eax`.

**D3DDevice_SetTexture (Stage, pTexture) — 0x306460.**
- eax = Stage: kept in esi (0x306470). Indexes device+0xF88+stage×4 (0x306473/0x3064A6); 0xF88 is CDevice_Textures in
  5849. Also used for method `0x41B0C + stage<<6` at 0x3064C5.
- stack = pTexture: `[esp+0x14]` at 0x30646B, after sub 8 + 2 pushes, is the first slot. AddRef at 0x3064FF. `ret 4`
  at 0x3064FC.
- Callers: 0x12A56 `push eax` (texture), `mov eax,esi` (loop stage counter); 0x144FE `push 0`, `mov eax,2`.

**D3DDevice_SetTextureState_TexCoordIndex (Stage, Value) — 0x304310.** Identity: uses the 5849 signature constants
0x41964, 0xC03C, 0x8511, 0x8512, 0x2400, 0x2401, 0x950 and 0x47F.
- esi = Stage: `esi<<7` indexes the texture state table at 0x3149A0 (0x30431D–0x304322). 32 dwords per stage =
  TextureState_Stage in 5849. Also used for methods `0x41964 + stage*4` (0x304360) and `(0xC03C+stage)<<4` (0x3043BC).
- stack = Value: `[esp+0x10]` at 0x304319, after 3 pushes. Stored at 0x304322; its high word selects the TCI mode at
  0x304351–0x304381. `ret 4` at 0x304412.
- Callers: 0x3045A5–0x3045AD (generic texture-state setter, type 0x1C) `push ebx` (value), `mov esi,eax` (stage).
  0x722F2 `push ebp`, `xor esi,esi`. 0x7231E `push ebx`, `mov esi,ebx`.
- Cross-check: 0x3149A0 = 0x314930 + 0x1C×4, the same table the generic setter writes at 0x30459C.

**D3DDevice_SetTransform (State, pMatrix) — 0x3060E0.**
- eax = State: `state<<6` + device+0xC60 (0x3060E8–0x3060EE); 0xC60 is CDevice_Transforms in 5849. 0x30610D compares
  it with 1 (projection).
- edx = pMatrix: 16 dwords are copied from it (0x3060FA–0x3060FC); floats read from it at 0x30611C onward.
- Plain `ret` at 0x3061ED/0x30623D.
- Callers: 0x71290 `mov edx,[esp+4]`, `mov eax,[0x4527D4]`, then the caller's own `ret 4` shows the callee popped
  nothing. 0x7158F `lea edx,[esp]` (local matrix).

**D3DDevice_Swap (Flags) — 0x309660.**
- eax = Flags: kept in ebx (0x309668). 0 is replaced by 5 at 0x309688; tested with `& 3` at 0x309695.
- Increments device+0x2478 (0x3096BE/0x309700) and returns it (0x309741). 0x2478 is CDevice_SwapCount in 5849.
- Plain `ret` at 0x30974A; the next function starts at 0x309750.
- Callers: 0xACFFF, 0xAF299, 0xAF2B3, 0xAF555 all `xor eax,eax` then call.

**D3DResource_IsBusy (pResource) — 0x3080B0.** Identity: exact `.XTLID` 0x8006F match.
- ecx = pResource: `[ecx]` common at 0x3080B0, the surface parent at `[ecx+0x14]` (0x3080C9), and the 0x780000
  refcount tests. These are the same checks the HLE performs.
- Plain `ret` at 0x30812A/0x308131.
- Caller: 0xBE03E `mov ecx,[ebp+0x410]`, nothing pushed, return tested at 0xBE04D.

**D3D_InternalRelease (pResource) — 0x307F30.**
- esi = resource: 0x307F30 `[esi]` checks the internal count 0x80000. 0x307F80 subtracts 0x80000; a surface also
  releases its parent `[esi+0x14]` at 0x307F4F. When nothing holds it, it frees through 0x307E80.
- Plain `ret` at 0x307F7F/0x307F88.
- Callers (inside D3D): 0x305A94 `mov esi,[ebp+0x1A04]` (render target) and 0x305ABE `mov esi,[ebp+0x1A08]` (depth),
  both then `call`. Also 0x30C44F and 0x30C479.

**D3D_MakeRequestedSpace (MinimumSpace, RequestedSpace) — 0x30A170.**
- eax = RequestedSpace: kept in ebx (0x30A17B). `push + requested + 0x4000` is checked against the push buffer end
  (0x30A1B5, 0x30A1CA–0x30A1D0).
- stack = MinimumSpace: `[esp+0x1C]` at 0x30A1D4, after sub 8 + 4 pushes. `push + minimum` is the fallback check
  (0x30A1D8–0x30A1DC). `ret 4` at 0x30A1AA.
- Callers (167): e.g. 0x12996 `mov eax,[0x3181A4]` / `shr edx` / `push edx`; the stack always gets the smaller value.
  In DrawVertices (0x3083E6–0x308404) eax = max(full, need) and the pushed ecx = max(half, need).
- Which slot is "minimum" rests on the API order (MinimumSpace, RequestedSpace) plus the smaller-value evidence. The
  HLE ignores both arguments, so this assignment cannot affect behaviour.

## Side findings (outside this task, for the layout work)

These are candidates seen in passing. They are not checked against every use.

- `D3D_DirtyFlags` = 0x316FF0. SetPixelShader 0x304F98 and SetTexture 0x3064ED or 0x4800 into it; 0x4800 is
  DirtyFlags_Texture in 5849.

  and 0x304322. This name-to-address mapping is unverified.

  0x7A0 IndexBuffer, 0xC60 Transforms, 0xF88 Textures, 0x2478 SwapCount.
